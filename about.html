HOW IT STARTED

Built Around One Simple Belief

Pets are part of the family. They bring comfort, energy, companionship, and plenty of unforgettable moments into our lives.

We created Paws & Whiskers to make one small part of pet parenting easier: finding useful, enjoyable products in one welcoming place.

From everyday food and tasty treats to toys and other essentials, our collection is built around the things cats and dogs actually need to feel comfortable, happy, and loved.

Our Promise

Keep pet shopping simple, thoughtful, and enjoyable.
